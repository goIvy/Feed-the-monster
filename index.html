What you'll build

Skills that outlast the trophy.

Competition is the practice field. What students take with them is the ability to think clearly, argue fairly, and speak with confidence in any room.

Research

Find credible evidence fast, separate strong sources from weak ones, and know a topic well enough to defend either side of it.

Argument

Build a case, anticipate the response, and answer it under pressure. Learn to disagree with an idea while respecting the person.

Delivery

Voice, pace, posture, and presence. Practice until speaking in front of a crowd feels less like a test and more like a conversation.

From first speech to finals

How a season runs.

No experience needed. Most of our competitors had never given a speech before their first practice.

  1. Late summer

    Open mic night

    Give a 60-second speech about anything. Everybody goes. Nobody's graded.

  2. Early fall

    Skills lab

    Weekly workshops on structure, delivery, research, and taking notes on an argument.

  3. Fall

    Novice tournaments

    Your first rounds against other first-years, with a coach debriefing every ballot.

  4. Winter

    Varsity circuit

    Travel tournaments, bigger fields, elimination rounds, and speaker awards.

  5. Spring

    Qualifiers & nationals

    Compete for a spot at the state and national championships.
